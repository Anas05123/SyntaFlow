# ADR-006: Delivery Gate Enforcement on Approved Deliverables

> **Date:** 2026-08-11  
> **Status:** Accepted  
> **Scope:** Architecture & Domain Governance  

---

## Context
Premature delivery of unapproved assets damages agency credibility and creates legal exposure.

## Decision
Delivery packages strictly enforce approval gates: all included deliverables must have approved review decisions before final delivery can be marked complete.

## Consequences
Eliminates accidental shipment of unvetted or rejected work.
