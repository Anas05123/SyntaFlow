# ADR-009: Restrained Graphite Palette & Controlled Accents

> **Date:** 2026-08-15  
> **Status:** Accepted  
> **Scope:** Architecture & Domain Governance  

---

## Context
Flashy gradients, neon borders, and SaaS dashboard clutter induce cognitive fatigue for solo operators working 8+ hours a day.

## Decision
Implement a bespoke dark graphite palette (#0B0D0F, #12161A), restrained cobalt/cyan accents (#3B82F6, #06B6D4), and authentic materials.

## Consequences
Calm, focused, studio-grade aesthetic aligned with premium operational command.
