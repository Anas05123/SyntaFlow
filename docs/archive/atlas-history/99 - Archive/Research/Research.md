# Archived Research

Store historical research here when it is no longer active.

Active research belongs in [[../../12 - Research|12 - Research]].

Research may be archived when:

- A tool was rejected
- A benchmark was superseded
- A model recommendation changed
- A provider is no longer supported
- A technical alternative was reviewed and closed

Archived research must include its original date, reason for archival, and any replacement research link.
