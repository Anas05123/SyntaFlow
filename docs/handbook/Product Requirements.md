# Product Requirements

## Version 1 Objectives

- Local desktop application.
- Campaign management.
- Multi-source business discovery.
- Business enrichment.
- Transparent opportunity scoring.
- AI-assisted website generation.
- Website preview and revision history.
- CRM and outreach tracking.
- Deployment preparation and export.
- Atlas AI assistant.
- Local settings, backup, and recovery.

## Explicitly Outside Version 1

- Mandatory cloud accounts.
- SaaS billing.
- Multi-user collaboration.
- Fully autonomous outreach.
- Public plugin marketplace.
- Mobile application.
